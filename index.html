NTHAKA DZUKA

"Khama Lanu, Tsogolo Lanu"

Tikupatsirani feteleza wabwino kwambiri wa NPK kuti mbewu zanu zikule bwino ndikukupatsirani zokolola zochuluka. Feteleza wathu amathandiza nthaka kukhala yabwino.

Onani Feteleza Wathu

Feteleza Wathu Wapamwamba

NPK 50kg (Feteleza Wathunthu)

Feteleza wothandiza kuti mbewu zanu zikhale zobiriwira, zikule mwamphamvu ndikupanga zipatso zambiri. Ndi woyenera ku mbewu zosiyanasiyana monga chimanga, fodya, ndi masamba.


Mtengo: K65,000

Feteleza Wamadzi (Liquid Fertilizer)

Wothandiza kwambiri pa masamba ndi mbewu zazing'ono. Umaugwira ntchito mwachangu ndipo umathandiza kuti mbewu zizolowereka msanga ku nthaka.


Mtengo: K25,000

Urea (Feteleza wa Nitrogen)

Wabwino kwambiri pakukulitsa masamba a mbewu. Amapangitsa kuti mbewu zikhale zobiriwira ndikuthandiza pakupanga kwa mapuloteni mu mbewu.


Mtengo: K55,000
